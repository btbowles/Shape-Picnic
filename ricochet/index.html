How to play

Launch from a lettered opening. Set up the mirrors so your last ball exits through the green finish.

  • ╱ A gold gate redirects the ball by 90° and turns. Strike it the same way again and the ball leaves in the opposite direction.
  • ╱ A blue mirror redirects without turning.
  • ⊞ A bumper or closed edge sends the ball back.

Use letter keys to launch. R resets the level.
